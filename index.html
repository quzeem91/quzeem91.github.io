About Me

Hello, Quzeem Olamide here. I'm a Data & BI Analyst focused on turning business data into reliable dashboards, actionable insights, and scalable reporting solutions. My work sits at the intersection of data analysis, business intelligence, and reporting automation.

I work primarily with Power BI, SQL, Microsoft Excel, DAX, Power Query, and Power Pivot to transform data into meaningful business insights. I also have hands-on experience with Microsoft Fabric and modern analytics workflows, with a focus on building solutions that are practical, maintainable, and useful to decision-makers.

Beyond building analytical solutions, I actively share my knowledge through technical sessions, webinars, tutorials, and articles covering Power BI, Excel, and business intelligence. I enjoy breaking down technical concepts and showing how they can be applied to real reporting and analytical problems.

I am a 3× Microsoft Certified professional, holding certifications in Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, and a graduate of the University of Lagos with a degree in Mathematics and Education.
